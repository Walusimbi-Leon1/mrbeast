The Story Behind the Screen

Jimmy Donaldson — better known to the world as MrBeast — is the most subscribed individual on YouTube, a serial entrepreneur, and one of the most influential philanthropists of the digital age.

From counting to 100,000 in a single video to planting 20 million trees, from launching a chocolate empire to creating the biggest reality show in streaming history, his journey is unlike anything the internet has ever seen.

This is the full story — the struggles, the breakthroughs, the billions, and the boy who never stopped believing that the next video could change everything.
