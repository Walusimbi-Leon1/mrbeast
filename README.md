## About the Book

*MrBeast* is a comprehensive biography of Jimmy Donaldson — known as MrBeast — the biggest YouTuber on the planet. From making videos in his bedroom at age 12 to building a media empire worth billions, this book traces the complete journey of the man who redefined what it means to create content.

### What's Inside

- **Early Life** — Growing up in North Carolina, discovering YouTube as a kid
- **The YouTube Journey** — From worst intros to counting to 100,000
- **Content Evolution** — How he turned stunts into an art form
- **The Team** — Building an empire with the best creators in the world
- **Beast Philanthropy** — Planting 20 million trees, cleaning oceans, feeding the hungry
- **Business Empire** — Feastables, Beast Burger, and the business of content
- **Record-Breaking Feats** — His most insane and ambitious videos
- **Famous Quotes** — 50+ memorable quotes with context

- **14 chapters** of narrative biography
- **50+ famous quotes** with attribution and context
